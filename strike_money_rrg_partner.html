Mastering Sector Rotation with RRG

RRG maps relative strength (RS-Ratio) and momentum (RS-Momentum) onto a coordinate space. Leading assets rotate clockwise across these core stages:

1

Improving Quadrant

Underperforming sectors are building dynamic momentum. This is the optimal window to build early exposure in emerging leaders.

2

Leading Quadrant

Fully established uptrends with strong momentum. Stocks here are actively driving market returns and crushing benchmarks.

3

Weakening Quadrant

Uptrends are cooling down. Price action might consolidate or stagnate; a warning sign to scale back or lock in profits.
